# 081 - Discrete fluid levels and stable flow (Terraria-style simulation)

**Priority:** P1 - **Status:** Done (2026-10-17) - **Part of:** fluid overhaul epic (081-084) - **Depends on:** 052, 076

## Problem
`FluidGrid` is the classic W-Shadow continuous-mass automaton (`FLUID_MAX_COMPRESSION`, `stableBottom`). It conserves fluid well, but its state is noisy, and that noise is what the renderer has to fight:
- **Endless trickles.** Levels are floats and water's `minLevel` is 0.02, so a pool beside a drop keeps leaking a sliver over the edge for a long time. Each sliver is drawn as a 1/8-tile stick (the "random bar along the cliff" in `FLUID_AUDIT.md`).
- **Pulsing "falling".** `fallen` is "what fell in during the last step", so a steady stream reads as falling one step and not the next. The client then needs `hurry`, `fallHurry`, a slide (`fallProgress`), blob mode and gap bridging to hide it (all in 082/083's scope to delete).
- **Mounds at the foot of a fall.** Landing water arrives faster than it levels out (partly fixed by the `roomUnder` ramp, but still visible).

Reference: Terraria (2D, side view, finite fluid that stays put, lava + water -> obsidian like our lavarock). Copy how it **behaves**, not how it looks (that is 082/083, Minecraft-style).

## Design (confirmed 2026-10-17; Decisions below override anything here)
- **Integer units per cell.** A full cell is `FLUID_UNITS` units (16). No pressure (see Decisions). Total fluid stays exactly the same (integers, no float drift), apart from reactions, `drainFluid`, evaporating films and squeezing out of the world, which are all counted.
- **Explicit falling state, not a per-step pulse.** After each step a cell is `falling` when the cell below it is open and not full (or is itself falling). It is a stable fact of the grid: a steady waterfall is falling every step. Sent as one bit with the level.
- **Down first:** everything that fits drops at once (as now).
- **Sideways only when the difference is at least 2 units**, moving half the difference (rounded down) at the fluid's flow speed. A 1-unit difference is level, so a pool stops completely instead of shuffling slivers back and forth forever. **Pouring over an edge** (a neighbour with room under it) moves the whole difference, so a ledge pool drains cleanly instead of dribbling.
- **Landing spreads fast:** fluid that fell into a cell this step spreads sideways in the same step it lands (the Terraria/Minecraft feel), so falls do not build a wedge.
- **Flow speed stays data:** `flowRate` becomes "how much of a difference moves per step" in units (thick fluids like lava move fewer units, or only every few steps). No fluid key in logic.
- **Thinnest amount is 1 unit.** `minLevel` is no longer used, so the evaporating-film problem and its leak guards go away (lone 1-unit films evaporate on a timer instead; see Decisions).
- **Unchanged:** density swap, reactions (`costPerLevel`, `ratePerStep`, run at the start of a step), `drainFluid`, `onTileChanged`, active-cell set, determinism, step rate (`FLUID_STEP_TICKS`), fog-filtered deltas.
- **Wire:** level byte = units (0-16), and a falling bit. `fallen` amounts go away. Submersion, fire, sounds and contact damage read `level / FLUID_UNITS` through the same getters, so they need no rule changes.
- **Data:** existing `Fluid` rows convert their float `flowRate`/`minLevel`/`flowThreshold` at load (no DB edit needed), or with a dry-run script if the admin fields change meaning (decide in Q&A). Fluid pockets in map generation fill whole cells, so they are unaffected.

## Decisions (Q&A 2026-10-17)
- **16 units per full cell** (`FLUID_UNITS = 16`). Thinnest amount is 1 unit.
- **Pressure is dropped** (Terraria-style): no compression, no "up" flow, no `stableBottom`, no U-bend levelling. A cell never holds more than 16 units, so the wire byte is plain units 0-16. Delete `FLUID_MAX_COMPRESSION` and its tests. Fluid squeezed by a tile being placed still goes to neighbours via `onTileChanged`, and anything left over is lost and counted, as now.
- **Lone 1-unit films slowly evaporate.** A cell holding exactly 1 unit that is resting (not falling) and has no same-fluid neighbour (left/right/below) with 2 or more units starts a per-cell age. After `FLUID_FILM_EVAPORATE_STEPS` (start at 100 steps = 10 s; a shared constant, not per fluid) it empties and is counted in `evaporatedVolume`. Any change to the cell resets the age. Deterministic (step counts, no randomness). Gotcha: such a cell has settled and leaves the active set, so keep a separate `films` set that is checked each step (cheap, and only film cells are in it). Otherwise films never age.
- `minLevel` and `flowThreshold` are no longer used: hidden in the admin, DB columns untouched (see the implementer notes).
- Falling speed is unchanged (one cell per fluid step, 10 tiles/s).

## Notes for the implementer (gotchas found while writing this ticket)
- **Keep the public API in cell fractions (0..1), store units inside.** `getLevel` must still return `units / FLUID_UNITS`. Every reader expects 0..1 and must not change: `MiningFireSubsystem.fuelAt`, `MiningIgnitionSubsystem`, `MiningMobSubsystem` (spawn check), `fluidSubmersion`, `fluidForces`, `FluidGlow`, the sandbox, the gas eviction. `setCell` / `addFluid` / `placeFluid` also take fractions from callers (map generation passes 1, `SandboxTools.pourFluid` passes `1 - level`, the admin preview `POUR_RATE = 0.5`). Convert at the boundary with `Math.round(level * FLUID_UNITS)`, and refuse or ignore amounts that round to 0.
- **Fire drains tiny fractions.** `FireGrid` calls `consumeFuel(x, y, seconds / burnDuration)`, which is about 0.01 of a cell per fire step. At 16 units that rounds to 0, and burning oil or lava would never burn out. Give `drainFluid` a per-cell fractional remainder (e.g. a `Float32Array drainDebt`): add the amount, take whole units once it reaches 1, and clear it when the cell empties or changes fluid. `drainedVolume` keeps counting in fractions. Add a test: 100 small drains of 0.01 empty a full cell.
- **Reactions use fractions too.** `costPerLevel` (default 2, admin-editable, may be non-integer) and `ratePerStep` (0.25). Convert to units with the same remainder approach, or round the per-step amounts and keep the remainder, so the 2:1 ratio holds over time. `fluidReactions.test.ts` must still pass unchanged in meaning (adjust only the exact numbers).
- **Slow fluids must still move.** Lava's `flowRate` is 0.06. `floor(diff / 2 * 0.06)` is 0 for any difference up to 16 units, so lava would never spread. Make slow fluids spread at least 1 unit, every N steps instead of every step: `N = max(1, round(1 / (flowRate * k)))` with `k` tuned so water (0.6) spreads every step. Gate it with `stepCount % N`, never randomness. Pick `k` so water feels like today and lava creeps.
- **Pressure is dropped (decided).** Remove `FLUID_MAX_COMPRESSION`, `stableBottom`, the "up" flow and their tests, and the "squeezed past full" comments. The U-bend test becomes "water fills the low part of a U-bend and stops". `FluidField.getLevel`'s "0 to about 2" comment becomes 0 to 1.
- **Falling cells must not spread sideways in mid-air.** Only a cell that rests (solid below, or a full non-falling cell below) spreads sideways. The current code has a comment about the bug this avoids: one poured cell fanned out into thin separate columns.
- **"Landing spreads in the same step" fights the order.** Rows are processed bottom-first, so the landing cell was already processed before the fluid arrives. Do a second, small sideways pass over the cells that received a fall this step, or spread from the landing cell when the fall is sent. Don't reverse the whole order.
- **Keep the "both sides from the same base" rule.** Work out both sideways flows from the same starting amount (see the comment in the current `step`). With integer half-differences, an odd unit should go to the side the alternating order favours. Doing the sides one after the other made falls pulse.
- **Work out "falling" bottom-up per column after the flows:** `falling(x, y) = !solid(x, y+1) && (units(x, y+1) < FLUID_UNITS || falling(x, y+1))`, for cells holding fluid. A pool draining through a hole is truly falling above the hole; that is fine. Report a cell in `changed` when its falling bit flips, as the current code does for `fallen`.
- **Wire format:** `FLUID_LEVEL_STEPS` (128) becomes `FLUID_UNITS` (16), with no compression. The optional 5th tuple value becomes a 0/1 falling flag, not an amount. `FluidField.falling` becomes a flag, `getFalling` returns 0 or 1, and `FluidLevels.getFalling` stays optional. Update `fluidWire.test.ts` and `fluidFallingWire.test.ts`. The server only sends fog-revealed cells: keep `takeVisibleChanges` / `visibleSnapshot` untouched apart from the encoding.
- **Admin fields.** `minLevel` and `flowThreshold` are no longer used by the simulation (1 unit and the 2-unit rule replace them). Leave the DB columns alone (no schema change, no data script). Hide the two fields in `FluidDetail.tsx`, stop the admin preview passing them, and leave `fluid.controller.ts` accepting them so old clients don't fail. `flowRate` keeps its 0..1 slider; update its hint text. Never run `prisma db seed`. `packages/shared/src/data/fluids.json` is only the dev mirror and does not need edits.
- **`FluidSoundProfile`, `FluidBodies`, `fluidForces`, `fluidSubmersion` tests** read levels through the sampler. They should pass unchanged, except where they place fluid with exact floats that no longer round cleanly (prefer multiples of 1/16 in tests).
- **Old tests to retire:** `fluidFalling.test.ts`, `fluidFallingWire.test.ts` and `fluidPourRegression.test.ts` test the old `fallen` amounts and pour heuristics. Rewrite what still applies (wire round trip, a pour reaching the floor) and delete the rest. Don't leave skipped tests.
- Run `npx tsc` in `packages/shared` after each change (apps consume `dist`), then the server and client tests.

## Implementation notes (2026-10-17)
- **Shared `FluidGrid`** rewritten on whole units: `units: Uint8Array` (0-16), `FLUID_UNITS`, `FLUID_SPREAD_MIN_DIFF = 2`, `FLUID_FILM_EVAPORATE_STEPS = 100`. Public API unchanged in meaning (fractions in and out); new: `getUnits`, `isFalling`, `filmCount`. `getFalling` now returns the cell's level while falling, else 0. `FLUID_MAX_COMPRESSION`, `FLUID_MIN_FLOW`, `stableBottom` and the "up" flow are gone.
- **A step is two passes**: (1) down, bottom row first, everything that fits drops at once; (2) sideways for fluid that *rests* (fluid with room below falls on, it never fans out in mid-air), worked out from the post-fall state, so landing fluid spreads in the same step. Both sides come from the same amount; the side order alternates every step; odd units are trimmed from the last side.
- **Slow fluids**: spreading is `floor(diff x share x flowRate)`; below one unit it moves one unit every `ceil(1/effective)` steps (`stepCount % period`, never random) and the cell stays awake (`waiting`) until its turn.
- **Falling flag** is worked out bottom-up per column after the flows (and straight away for `setCell`/`addFluid`/`drainFluid`/`onTileChanged`, so a cell placed in mid-air is told as falling at once). A flip sends the cell as changed, as does a level change.
- **Films**: a lone 1-unit film (no same-fluid left/right/below neighbour holds 2 or more) ages in a `films` map and evaporates (`evaporatedVolume`). `activeCount` does not count them (a pool with only a film is "settled"); `filmCount` does, and the scene runner's `settle()` waits for both.
- **Not in the ticket, added after measuring** (see scene tests): spreading stops at a 1-unit difference, which left a gentle staircase (11,10,10,9,8 across five cells). `levelQuietRows` deals out a resting row of one fluid evenly (cells differ by one unit at most) once nothing in it moved this step and nothing is held back. The fuller cells keep the extra units, so nothing shuffles sideways.
- **Fire/drain**: `drainFluid` keeps a per-cell remainder (`drainDebt`) so fire's 0.01-cell pieces add up; reset when the cell is emptied or set. **Reactions**: whole units with a per-cell carry (`reactionCarry`): water is spent in units, lava hardens as the carry adds up to whole units (2 water per lava unit holds over time); `ratePerStep` caps lava units per step.
- **Wire**: `FLUID_LEVEL_STEPS = FLUID_UNITS` (16); the fifth value is a 0/1 falling flag (`toFluidWireCell`). `FluidField` stores a flag; `getFalling` is the level while falling, `isFalling` is new on `FluidLevels` (optional).
- **Admin**: `minLevel` / `flowThreshold` are hidden in the Fluid form (columns, API and old rows untouched; the grid ignores them; `FluidParams` makes them optional). `flowRate` hint reworded. No data script, no DB change.
- **Tests**: `FluidGrid.test.ts` (38), `fluidFalling*.test.ts`, `fluidWire.test.ts` rewritten for units; scene tests (084). Server wire tests expect the falling flag; `MiningGameEngine.burning.test.ts` moves the mob clear of oil, which now spreads thinly (to x 12) along the whole hall. Mutation-checked: the 2-unit threshold, film evaporation, drain remainder, falling rule, quiet-row levelling and slow-fluid movement each fail tests.
- **Behaviour changes to know about**: a poured cell landing on a floor now spreads into a flat puddle and its 1-unit edge films evaporate (about 1/16 of a cell is lost, counted). Oil and other thin fluids spread further than before (no minimum level); watch spilled-oil fires.
