# 123 - Conveyor unloading and item travel

**Priority:** P1 - **Status:** Done in code (judge the feel in the game) - **Depends on:** 121, 122

## Decisions (2026-10-22, from the owner)
- **Touching the belt starts the unload, for as long as they are touching it.** The belt does not move the player.
- Items unload **one at a time**, onto the belt, and **travel up it into the extractor**. Like other games: visible items spaced on the belt, a full belt backs up the unload.
- Rates (`beltSpeed`, `unloadItemsPerSecond`) are on the extractor definition.

## Proposed design
- A player is "touching" when their collider overlaps any belt cell. While touching, every `1/unloadItemsPerSecond` seconds one item moves from the backpack to the belt at that cell, if there is room at that spot (minimum spacing between items).
- Order: heaviest first, ties oldest first (decided 2026-10-22, owner); this empties weight fastest.
- Belt items are simulated server-side as positions along the belt (1D), moving up at `beltSpeed`; at the top they enter the stash of the player who loaded them. Each item keeps its owner.
- Items on the belt are **not safe** until they reach the extractor: death, timeout or a cut belt (124) loses them.
- Client: items render on the belt (Pixi sprites, item icon scaled to belt size) and travel up with interpolation, one pooled sprite per item, camera-culled. Sound (belt loop while running, per-item plink) via the sound library with per-slot volume.
- Wire: item spawn and arrival as events (id, item, position, owner), positions derived on the client from speed, not sent per tick.

## Tests
Unload rate, one at a time, spacing and backup, order, touching starts/stops, items reach the stash with owner, lost on cut/death, client interpolation. Mutation: save items at spawn on the belt -> death test fails.

## Art
The images are generated by Gemini (owner task, ticket 128); use placeholders until they exist.

## Progress (2026-10-22)
- Server (`MiningBeltSubsystem`): a player whose body overlaps a laid belt cell hands over one unit every `1/unloadItemsPerSecond` seconds, heaviest item first (`nextUnloadIndex`), onto the belt where they stand. A spot with an item closer than the new `beltItemSpacing` (extractor data, default 1.5 cells) refuses, which is how a busy belt backs the unload up. Standing still on a full spot loads at most one at a time.
- Items ride up in 1D at `beltSpeed`, all at the same speed, so spacing never closes and the client can work out positions from the start alone. At the top the item goes to its OWNER's stash (`creditStash`: live stash if they are still in the room, straight to the database if they left). Items on the belt are not safe: when a player leaves the room (death, timeout, exit) their items are removed.
- Wire: `beltItems: { added, removed }` in the tick, `beltItems` in the join state.
- Client: `BeltItemRenderer` (one view per item, its icon scaled to the belt, moving by speed from where the server said it started; a dot until the icon loads), `ExtractorSounds` (new extractor slot `unload` at each item's spot, and the existing `loop` slot as a running hum while anything is on the belt, each with its own volume).
- Mutation check: crediting the stash when an item goes on the belt makes the death test fail.
- A cut belt losing items is ticket 124.
