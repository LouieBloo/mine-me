# 124 - Belt damage: mobs and fire

**Priority:** P2 - **Status:** Done in code (mob belt-seeking and break particles left) - **Depends on:** 122, 123

## Decisions (2026-10-22, from the owner)
- Mobs can attack and damage the belt, and fire can burn or damage segments.
- **Segments have health; a broken segment stops the line.** Items on a cut belt below the break are lost. The extractor itself is not destructible.

## Proposed design
- Belt segments are targets in the unified damage pipeline (`applyDamage`/`damageTile`), health from `beltSegmentHealth` on the extractor definition.
- Mob AI treats belt cells as an attackable target when blocked or when it's the nearest target (a flag in `aiConfig`, data, not a hard-coded mob).
- Fire: belts take damage from the fire system through the shared `Flammability` shape (a belt "block" definition with `heat` and no `burnsInto`), and from fluid contact damage (lava).
- A broken segment: the line below it stops, items beyond it are lost; the belt is rebuilt over time by the build subsystem (122) as it re-lays the missing segment (rebuild uses the same per-segment rate).
- Visual: damaged segments show cracks; breaking plays a sound and particles from libraries.

## Progress (2026-10-22)
- A belt is a damage target in the unified pipeline: `DamageTarget` gained `{ kind: 'belt', id: segment index }`, handled in `MiningDamageSystem.applyDamage` (to `MiningBeltSubsystem.damageSegment`). Health per segment is the extractor's `beltSegmentHealth`; the extractor itself is not a target (it has no handler, and `health` is only data).
- Sources: **mobs** with `aiConfig.attacksBelt: true` hit the nearest segment in attack range (when no player or block is in reach) with their own Damage effect; **fire, heat and harmful fluid (lava)** through the new `MiningBurningSubsystem.hazardRate` (the same fire rule, heat and fluid-contact numbers bodies use), checked every 0.25 s; **blasts** by distance like everything else in the radius.
- A broken segment (`null` in the list) cuts the line: items riding at or below it are lost, nothing can be put on the belt below the break, and the belt lays the segment again before it digs further, at the build rate, waiting while fluid, gas, fire or a rock is in the gap.
- Wire: `stages` (0 whole to 4, -1 broken) when any change, and `broke` (indices) for the break sound; the client draws cracks and a gap (placeholder look), and the new extractor sound slot `beltBreak` plays where a segment breaks.

(2) `attacksBelt` has no admin form: there is no editor for a mob's `aiConfig` at all, so set it on the mob row for now. (3) Break particles: the sound is wired; a particle effect for breaking was not added.
