# 143 - Doodads can be hurt and killed; ceiling doodads fall

**Priority:** P2 - **Status:** Built 2026-10-26, waiting for you to try it - **Depends on:** 099, 100, 101, 102, 105, 106, falling rocks (047, `MiningRockSubsystem`), the damage pipeline (011)

## Problem
Doodads were decided to be untouchable scenery (099: "never interactable"), and only the wiggle (104) and anchor removal (105) react to the player. That is wrong for the creatures: a cave beetle or firefly should die when a bullet, axe swing, dynamite blast or falling rock hits it, like any mob. A stalactite whose block is mined should not just vanish; it should fall like a rock and hurt whoever is under it. This ticket replaces the "never interactable" rule in 099 and the "removal only" rule in 105.

## Decisions (2026-10-26, from the owner)
- **Per doodad, not global.** Each doodad has an optional **Health** (admin field, stored on the `Doodad` row). Blank = it cannot be hurt (grass, mushrooms keep working as today). Set for the beetle, firefly and whatever else the owner wants breakable. Any doodad can be made breakable later by filling the field.
- **Hit shape = its footprint box** (its width x height, at its current wander position for bugs), like a small mob. No separate hitbox field.
- **Every damage source applies**: bullets, melee and pickaxe swings, dynamite and gas explosions, falling rocks and falling stalactites, fire/burning if it applies to mobs. They go through the unified damage pipeline (011: `applyDamage` with a `DamageEvent`), so a new weapon needs no doodad code.
- **Dying works like a mob dying**: it plays its death sound (a new doodad sound slot, volume per slot like every other sound) and a death particle effect (optional `ParticleEffect` reference), is removed, and never comes back this run. A hit that does not kill plays a hit sound/flash (optional, same slots idea).
- **No rewards**: no drops, no XP, no kill credit.
- **Ceiling doodads fall.** Every doodad with the CEILING anchor (stalactite, dripping stalactite, icicle) falls when its anchor block is removed (mined, blasted, burned) **and also when its health runs out from hits** (so it has to have a health value). It acts like a falling rock: it drops, **hurts players and mobs in its path**, and **breaks itself when it lands** (or hits anything solid). Damage scales with mass, a number set per doodad (a small icicle hurts less than a big stalactite). It also hurts **other doodads** it falls through (a beetle or firefly under it can die; a doodad with no health is unharmed).
- Floor, wall and air doodads keep today's rule: they just vanish when their anchor goes (105).

## Proposed design
- **Data (`Doodad`)**: `health Int?`, `fallDamage Float?` (the mass/damage of the falling version; blank = a default for its footprint), `deathParticleEffectId`, and sound slots `hit` and `death` (plus the existing `break`). Ceiling doodads fall always, so no checkbox; `health` is separate (blank = shot-proof but still falls with its anchor). Shared `DoodadDefinition` type, validation, loader, wire (health only if the client needs to show damage), admin fields, JSON mirror, a dry-run script to give the first 12 doodads sensible values.
- **Server state**: `MiningDoodadSubsystem` keeps current health per live doodad (only for ones with health) and exposes a damage target the damage system can find by position/box, including a wandering bug's current spot. `MiningDamageSystem` gets a doodad branch next to the mob and player ones; every source that sweeps for mobs also asks for doodads (melee arc, bullet raycast, explosion radius, rock landing).
- **Falling**: when a CEILING doodad dies or loses its anchor, the subsystem hands it to the falling-rock system as a falling body with the doodad's size and mass; on landing it damages what it touches (players, mobs, doodads), plays the break sound/effect, and is gone. It is removed from the doodad list at the moment it lets go and appears as a falling body on the wire, so clients draw it dropping with its own sprite.
- **Wire**: a damage/death delta for doodads (like `doodadsRemoved` but with the cause), and a falling-stalactite entity (or the rock wire with a texture), only to players who had it revealed.
- **Client**: hit flash / small knockback wobble on the sprite, death effect and sound, falling stalactite drawn with its sprite and a ground-hit effect. Respect the fog like all doodad deltas.
- **Sandbox/admin**: sandbox tools can hurt or kill a placed doodad (and drop a stalactite) to reproduce bugs; admin preview shows hit and death.

## Open questions (assumed until answered)
- Starting numbers (assumed): cave beetle 10 HP, firefly 1 HP, stalactite / dripping stalactite / icicle 15 / 15 / 5 HP, everything else blank. Fall damage is tuned in the game.
- Whether a lit doodad (firefly) should flash when hit, and whether bugs should knock back or flee when hurt (assumed: flash only, no flee).
- Whether the player should be able to damage their own stalactites in co-op (assumed yes: friendly fire matches rocks and dynamite).
- Fluids on the ground (lava, acid) hurting doodads is out of scope for now.

## Tests
Each damage source (bullet, melee, explosion, falling rock) kills a doodad with health and spares one without it (mutation: skip the doodad branch in the explosion path -> its test fails); a wandering bug is hit at its current spot, not its placed one; death sends the delta and plays the effect; a ceiling doodad falls on anchor loss and on health 0, hurts a player and a mob below, kills a bug below, and is gone after landing; floor/wall/air doodads still just vanish; unseen doodads send nothing; admin fields validate; client draws the fall.

## Built (2026-10-26)
- **Data:** `Doodad.health` (Int?), `fallDamage` (Float?), `deathParticleEffectId` (relation `DoodadDeathParticle`); two new sound slots, `hit` and `death` (the `break` slot is unchanged: the block went). Shared type, validation (`health` whole and at least 1, `fallDamage` above 0), `toClientDoodad` sends only the death effect id. Admin: a new Damage panel (health, fall damage for ceiling doodads, death effect picker) and the two sounds in the existing sound picker. The controller accepts them (empty = none) and checks the effect exists.
- **Hurting:** `DamageTarget` has a `doodad` kind (id `"x,y"`, the top-left cell). `MiningDoodadSubsystem` keeps health per live doodad, only for ones with `health`, and answers `hurtableInBox` / `hurtableAlong` / `hurtableTargets`. Every source asks it: melee swing (not through a wall), bullets (nearest of mobs and doodads first; a bullet hits a doodad once), dynamite and gas explosions (by distance to the middle of the box, like mobs), falling rocks and falling doodads. Not done: fire and burning (they act on bodies, not cells).
- **Hit box (changed assumption):** wander is client-only, so the server does not know where a bug is. A wandering doodad's hit box is its footprint grown by `wander.radius`. A small bug can therefore be hit a little away from where it is drawn.
- **Death:** at 0 health the doodad is removed (`doodadsKilled` then `doodadsRemoved` in the same tick). A hit that does not kill sends `doodadsHit` (red tint, hit sound). Both go to everyone who had been told of the doodad, and only those.
- **Falling:** a CEILING doodad falls when its block goes (mined, blasted, burnt) or its health runs out. `MiningFallingDoodads` moves it with the pure shared `stepFall` (gravity, stops at the first solid row under any column of its width or at the map floor), hurts each player, mob and doodad it passes once per fall (speed damage by the rock rule x mass, `fallDamage` or the rock mass of its footprint), then shatters (no block is made). Wire: `fallingDoodads` (every tick, in view of the player) and `doodadsShattered` (death effect and sound where it landed). A ceiling doodad with no health can't be shot but still falls with its block.
- **Client:** `DoodadRenderer` draws falling doodads (not part of the field, so no reactions, light or sound while falling), tints hit ones, plays death (not break) for killed ones, and a ceiling doodad that loses its hold is not faded (the falling copy takes over). Hit/death listeners feed `DoodadSounds` (hit/death slots, spaced out) and `DoodadEffects.deathBurst` (the death effect at the middle of the doodad).
- **Sandbox:** a Hit doodad tool (`sandbox_hit_doodad`, 5 hit points a click through the damage system; a ceiling doodad with no health is let go, any other without health is refused).
- **Data script:** `prisma/set-doodad-health.ts` (dry run by default, never overwrites a health already set). Applied to the dev DB: cave beetle 10, firefly 1, stalactite 15, dripping stalactite 15, icicle 5. The mirror `doodads.json` has them too.
- **Tests:** shared `doodadDamage.test.ts` and `doodad.test.ts`; server `MiningGameEngine.doodadDamage.test.ts` (every source, every delta, falling, once per fall, weight), `MiningFallingDoodads.test.ts`, `MiningDoodadSubsystem.test.ts`, `doodadSandbox.test.ts`, `admin.doodad.test.ts`, `set-doodad-health.test.ts`; client renderer, sounds, effects, tick handler, sandbox panel and actions; admin damage panel and form. Mutation-checked: skipping the doodad branch in the damage system, in dynamite, gas, bullets, melee and rocks, the once-per-fall set, hits on doodads under a falling one, "ceiling falls on anchor loss", the client's death-vs-break and no-fade-for-ceiling rules, and `doodadFalls` each fail a test.
- **Not built:** admin preview hit/death, fire damage, re-spawning killed doodads (a killed one is gone for the run). Not tried in a real browser.
