# 122 - Conveyor belt: digging down and laying segments

**Priority:** P1 - **Status:** Done in code (judge the look in the game) - **Depends on:** 119, 120, 139

## Decisions (2026-10-22, from the owner)
- The belt **removes blocks in its path and lays belt segments**, straight down from the extractor, **3 blocks wide**.
- **Pauses at fluid, gas or lava until cleared** so the player has a reason to deal with them.
- The player digs to reach the belt; the belt is not carried to the player.
- Build speed comes from the extractor definition (upgrades later).

## Proposed design
- `MiningBeltSubsystem` (server): every `buildSecondsPerSegment` it advances one row: clears the 3-wide row below the tip using the shared removal path (blocks, `clearPieceSiblings` for pieces and rocks, doodads anchored there go via ticket 105), then lays a belt row. Reuse the piece machinery so a belt row is a piece with a size.
- It stops while the next row contains fluid, gas, fire or a falling rock, and resumes when clear. It also stops at the map bottom or an indestructible block, and reports why (`belt.blockedBy`) for the HUD.
- The shaft is a real open channel: players and mobs can walk or fall into it. Falling players take normal fall damage.
- Belt segments are cells with a `belt` flag stored through the existing cell/piece representation so wire, blast and burn code needs no special cases.
- Wire: belt length as a delta; client draws segments for revealed cells only (fog).
- Generation (096/110): the shaft column must not be forced through unreachable fluid pockets. It just pauses.

## Tests
Advances at the data rate, clears pieces and rocks whole, pauses and resumes at fluid/gas/fire, stops at bottom, fog-limited wire. Mutation: ignore fluid -> pause test fails.

## Art
The images are generated by Gemini (owner task, ticket 128); use placeholders until they exist.

## Progress (2026-10-22)
- `MiningBeltSubsystem` (server): after the extractor lands, one segment every `buildSecondsPerSegment` (extractor data, read live). A segment clears the `shaftWidth`-wide row under the tip through `completeMiningBlock` (no loot; rocks and pieces go whole, rocks above start to fall) and lays the segment. The shaft is centred under the machine (`ExtractorPlacement.shaftX`) and starts on the ground row.
- It waits (and says why: `blockedBy`) while the next row has fluid, gas, fire or a falling rock, and carries on by itself when that clears. `solid` (a blast-proof block such as the entrance) and `bottom` end it.
- The belt is NOT a tile: it is the segment list (`segments`, health per segment for 124). The shaft cells are ordinary empty cells, so bodies walk or fall in. Chosen over a new tile type so no block table row, tile enum value or autotile rule had to change; 124 adds damage on the segment list instead.
- Wire: `belt` in the join state, and `{ rows, blockedBy }` in the tick only when it changes. Client: `BeltRenderer` (moving stripes at the belt speed, art via new `beltTextureUrl` on the extractor), and a "Conveyor Belt" HUD line saying what it is waiting for.
- Not done: fog-limiting the belt wire. The cells the belt clears are sent to everyone as tile updates, so the shaft shows as soon as it is dug.
