# 070 - Explosive gases and the explosion front

**Priority:** P1 - **Status:** Done (2026-10-14); judge the look and the damage in the game - **Depends on:** 056, 057 (ignition), 067

## Problem
Hydrogen must blow up when ignited. The explosion should be near-instant, but the player should be able to **see it travel through the gas**.

## Design
- **Data, not code:** extend the shared `Flammability` shape with an optional `explosion` sub-object (`blastRadius`, `damage`, how damage scales with concentration, `flameEffectId`, `flameLight`, `frontSpeed`). A gas's `flammability` uses it. `MiningIgnitionSubsystem.flammabilityAt` stays the single place that answers "does this burn or explode?" and now also looks at gas cells. Anything that already ignites (torches, the `Ignites` effect, fire) sets off hydrogen with no new wiring. Nitrogen has null flammability.
- **Explosion front:** `MiningGasExplosionSubsystem` (kept out of `GasGrid` so the grid has no game rules). When an explosive gas cell ignites it joins the front. Each step the front advances `frontSpeed` cells (several per tick) through connected cells of explosive gas; every cell burns once, deals damage, and consumes its gas (`drainGas`). A pocket therefore detonates over a handful of ticks, almost instantly, as a travelling wave.
- **Damage:** reuses the existing blast / `MiningDamageSystem` path for entities. Damage scales with the cell's concentration, so a big dense pocket hurts far more than a wisp. **Entities only: no terrain damage for now** (owner decision); keep the blast call shaped so terrain damage can be switched on later by data.
- **Visible travel:** cells crossed by the front are reported as events (cell, tick) in the gas delta/an explosion event so the client draws a flash, particles (via `ParticleEffect` rows, never hard-coded) and a light on each cell as the front passes (`flameLight` through the shared light helper). No client-side simulation of the front.
- Burned hydrogen can leave the cell empty, or `burnsInto` another gas/fluid if the data says so.
- Friendly fire and god mode follow the existing damage rules (sandbox god mode applies).

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-14)
- Fast but visible: a front, not a single-frame blast.
- Damages entities only for now.
- Open question for later: a pressure wave that shoves gas and fire outward. Not in this ticket.

## Tests
Torch/`Ignites`/fire ignites hydrogen; nitrogen never explodes; the front advances `frontSpeed` cells per tick and consumes gas; a disconnected pocket is not set off; damage scales with concentration and respects radius; no tile is changed; hit events arrive for each crossed cell; god mode takes no damage; determinism. Mutation-check: make the front jump instantly, confirm the "travels over several ticks" test fails.

## Implementation notes
- **Data:** `Flammability.explosion?: ExplosionSettings` (`blastRadius`, `damage` per full cell, `frontSpeed` in whole cells per gas step, optional `minConcentration`, default 0.1), validated by `validateExplosion` inside `validateFlammability`. A gas with `flammability` but no `explosion` is just inert for now. The look of the blast is the gas's own `flameEffectId` (a particle effect, bursts once in each cell) and `flameLight` (a short flash), sent to clients as `GasClientDefinition.explosion` only for gases that explode. Hydrogen in the seed mirror: radius 3, damage 8 a cell, speed 3 (so a 12-cell pocket crosses in about 4 gas steps, 0.4 s), minimum 0.1, flash effect `pe_dynamite_explosion_flash` and a pale blue light. Nitrogen has none. Tune it all in the admin.
- **Shared:** `ExplosionFront` (pure, deterministic, `gameLogic/gas/ExplosionFront.ts`). Setting off a cell lines it up; each step runs `frontSpeed` sub-waves, each sub-wave detonates the lined-up cells, consumes their gas and lines up the four neighbours that still hold explosive gas. Every cell goes off once; thin gas neither goes off nor carries the front; unconnected gas is left alone; the front moves at the speed of the fastest gas in it.
- **Server:** `MiningGasExplosionSubsystem` (`ignite`, `canIgnite`, `flammabilityAt`, `update`/`step` on the gas beat as tick step 4.56, `takeVisibleDetonations`). Each step's detonated cells hurt every living player and mob in reach through the one damage pipeline as a single `explosive` hit per body per step: the sum, over the step's cells, of blast damage (`blastDamageAt`: damage x concentration, falling with distance), with the usual blast knockback and `ignoreInvulnerability` so every step of the front hurts. No terrain is touched (decision). Each detonated cell also sparks the flammable fluids and blocks around it, so a hydrogen blast lights an oil pool beside it.
- **What sets it off (all through the existing ignition path, so nothing names an item):** `MiningIgnitionSubsystem.emit` now routes a cell of explosive gas to the explosion subsystem (rolling the gas's own ignition chance x the source's strength) instead of the fire grid; that covers torches, items with the Ignites effect, blasts and burning bodies. Fire already burning (oil, blocks) sets off gas touching it, through a new gas-only `igniteGas` called for each burning cell on the fire step.
- **Wire and client:** `gasExplosions: [x, y, gasId][]` on the tick, revealed cells only (a hidden pocket going up never shows where it is). `GasRenderer.playExplosions` bursts the gas's effect in each cell and flashes its light for 350 ms; a long front is thinned to at most 40 bursts and 8 lights per tick. The player sees the wave because the cells arrive a few at a time as the front moves.
- **Admin:** `FlammabilityPanel` takes `allowExplosion` (used by the gas page): "Explodes when lit" with blast radius, damage per cell, front speed and minimum concentration; the flame settings are worded as the explosion's flash.
- **Not done here (by design):** terrain damage (the blast call is shaped so it can be added by data later), a pressure wave that shoves gas and fire outward (open question from the plan), sounds (073), sandbox ignition (072).
- **Tests:** shared `ExplosionFront.test.ts` (20), server `MiningGameEngine.gasExplosion.test.ts` (25), client explosion cases in `GasRenderer.test.ts` and `tickHandlers.test.ts`, admin cases in `FlammabilityPanel.test.tsx` and `GasDetail.test.tsx`. Mutation-checked: removing the ignition route (12 fail), fire lighting gas, invulnerability, concentration scaling, gas consumption, oil ignition, the fog rule, the handler call, the thinning and the light cleanup each fail a test.
