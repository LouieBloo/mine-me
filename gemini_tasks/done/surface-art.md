# Surface art (tickets 129-138, 137): overview

The surface above the ground works **without any picture**: the sky is a gradient, the sun, clouds, mountains, hills and treeline are drawn in code, and the trees, bushes and birds are coloured placeholder shapes. Each piece of art below replaces one of those drawn stand-ins, one at a time, as it is made. Nothing breaks if some are missing.

Make them as a set in one session so the palettes sit together, and follow `../ART_STYLE_GUIDE.md` (distant layers paler, bluer and more desaturated; nearer layers richer).

## Not needed (drawn in code)
- **The sun**: a soft glowing disc, set in the Surface Editor (colour, position, size).
- **Rain, snow, fog, splashes**: drawn in code from the weather.
- **Lightning**: a jagged bolt drawn in code, plus a flash of the sky light.
- **The EXIT sign** at the extraction point: drawn in code for now (a sign picture can be added later as a doodad).

## More environments (snowfield, dusty plains, forest...)
The same set with different prompts: change "green" to "white snow-capped" or "warm sand and red rock", and make them as separate strips and trees. Each environment is a row in the **Surface Editor**; pictures are uploaded per environment and layer.

## Tips
- Check **seamless tiling** of every strip: put two copies side by side and look for a join.
- Strips keep their bottom edge solid and everything above the ridge transparent.
- Upload only through the admin app (Surface Editor for strips and clouds, Doodads Editor for trees, bushes and birds); do not drop files into asset folders by hand.
- Record the prompts that worked in ticket 137 so the pictures can be regenerated.
